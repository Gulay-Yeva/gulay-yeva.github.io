WHY

Why engineering, and why energy

I chose engineering because I like work where details matter. I’m interested in systems where safety, procedure, and reliability are not optional. Energy systems feel real to me, because they have clear responsibility and clear impact.

Offshore environments interest me for the same reason. They are demanding. You need discipline and careful thinking, and you learn to respect standards. Renewable energy interests me because it is part of the future, and I want to understand how these systems are built and operated.

Studying for a full academic year in the United States as a FLEX scholarship student shaped how I work. It made me more independent and more serious about learning step by step.
